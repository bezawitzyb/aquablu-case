The vision
Aura is Aquablu's own platform. It started at the machines as a dashboard. Now it's the
operating layer for our installed base of dispensing systems, holding the telemetry, the service
state and the account data for every unit in the field. We're extending it into the place where
Aquablu itself runs: the one platform where everyone at the company works.
That's the destination. One place. Our data, our workflows, our product.
The problem
Today the company runs on a patchwork of external SaaS. Nobody's work lives in one place, a
single task routinely spans four tools, and the person doing it carries the context between them
in their head. Our own data sits outside our own product, which is an undesirable state.
We're going to fix this one department at a time.
The challenge
Customer Success is the first department. Work out how to get them working in Aura,
and start.
That's the whole brief. We're not going to tell you what CS does, which workflow to attack, what
to build, or what success looks like, figuring that out is the job, and how you go about it is what
we're actually interested in.
What to bring
A 15-minute presentation.
Show us what you built. Clickable, running, fake data is fine. Not a picture of a
product, but the actual thing.

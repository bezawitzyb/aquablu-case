## 1. The North Star (the goal everything else serves)

Aquablu's stated mission, printed on their own site and repeated in the BOLD launch: **"Hydrate a billion people to thrive every day."** The fuller sustainability version they use elsewhere: **hydrate a billion people and save a billion single-use plastic bottles before 2030.**

This is the frame every 2026 goal ladders up to. When you talk about their goals, anchor to this: everything they're doing in 2026 — new products, US entry, AI-native operations — is in service of scaling toward that billion-person number. It also reframes what "success" means for them: not just revenue, but *reach* and *plastic displaced*.

---

## 2. The Defining 2026 Strategic Shift: office-only → global hydration ecosystem

The single most important strategic statement for 2026 comes straight from the BOLD/CES press release: the CES presentation underscores Aquablu's growth and ambition by **"shifting focus from office-only solutions to a global hydration ecosystem."**

Unpack that — it's the spine of their whole 2026:
- **From B2B-only → B2B + consumer-facing.** Co-founder Marc van Zuylen: *"We want people to have access to hydration 24/7, wherever they are."* They're moving from "water for offices" to hydration anywhere — offices, gyms, hotels, airports, public spaces, and eventually the home.
- **From single-product → ecosystem.** Three legs now: **REFILL+ Series 2** (offices), **BOLD** (freestanding/public/consumer), and **AURA** (the connecting platform). The press release explicitly frames these three as the ecosystem.
- **From Netherlands/Europe → global.** CES 2026 in Las Vegas was Aquablu's **U.S. debut** — a deliberate signal that international expansion beyond Europe is now a live goal.

**For your interview:** this pivot is *the* thing to reference when asked "what do you know about where Aquablu is heading?" It's specific, it's from their own mouths, and it's recent.

## 3. The Verified 2026 Goals & Moves

### 3.1 Launch and scale the new product generation
- **REFILL+ Series 2 + AURA** launched Oct 2025 — described as their most significant product innovation to date; 2026 is the year to scale its rollout (Benelux/Germany/UK first, France + Scandinavia following).
- **BOLD** unveiled at CES Jan 2026 — currently on a **waitlist** (not yet broadly shipping), so **bringing BOLD to market is an active 2026 goal**, not a finished one.
- **AURA** is also still rolling out (its product page leads with a *waitlist*), so **maturing and scaling AURA is a live 2026 objective** — directly relevant to your role.

### 3.2 Enter the U.S. / go global
- CES was the U.S. debut, positioning the Netherlands as a leader in **"AI-driven water technology."** International expansion — especially the US — is a stated 2026 ambition.

### 3.3 Become (and operate as) an "AI-native" company
- Aquablu brands BOLD as an **"AI-driven hydration platform"** and itself as leading **AI-driven water technology**. Internally, their job ads repeat **"AI is how we build, not an afterthought."**
- This shows up in hiring: a **Senior AI Platform Engineer**, an **AI Solutions Engineer (Dylan)**, a **Senior Automation & AI Manager (Thomas)**, and the **Digital Product Builder** role itself (build with AI agents). **Embedding AI across product *and* internal operations is a 2026 goal** — and it's the goal your role most directly serves.

### 3.4 Grow the team deliberately (but stay lean)
- PitchBook lists **~88 employees** (2026); the team has scaled fast. Current open roles span engineering, product, operations, and (per ZoomInfo) **a Chief of Staff** and **Head of Quality & Compliance** — hires that signal a company **professionalizing its operations** as it scales.
- The widely-reported ambition (from founder interviews, and consistent with their lean model): reach **~€100M revenue with fewer than 100 employees by 2028.** *(Treat the exact figure as reported-not-official, but the philosophy — extreme efficiency, high revenue per head — is clearly real and shows up in how they hire.)* **This is why they want "builders" who ship with AI: headcount discipline is a strategic constraint.**

### 3.5 Deepen the platform / data advantage
- Scaling **AURA** as the connective tissue — real-time monitoring, predictive maintenance, auto-replenishment, sustainability reporting, and (with BOLD) personalization — is central. The efficiency goal (§3.4) is **only achievable if AURA automates operations**, so investing in the platform is a core 2026 priority. *(This is the strategic reason your CS-into-Aura business case matters: it's the same "run the company on our own platform" logic.)*

### 3.6 Keep the growth/financial trajectory
- Context they're building on: **profitable since April 2025**; **300%+ YoY growth three years running**; **#2 fastest-growing tech company in NL (Deloitte Fast50 2025)**; **#30 in Europe (Sifted 250)**; **€7M seed (2025) led by Frans van Houten, ex-Philips CEO.** The 2026 goal is to **sustain that hypergrowth** while adding the new products and markets above.

## 4. How the Goals Fit Together (the logic chain)

A clean way to hold it — and to explain it in interview:

> **North Star:** hydrate a billion people, save a billion bottles by 2030.
> → **To get there, in 2026:** expand from offices to a *global hydration ecosystem* (BOLD + US + consumer).
> → **To scale that efficiently:** stay lean (~€100M with <100 people) — which is *only* possible by being **AI-native** and running operations on **AURA**.
> → **Which is why they're hiring:** builders who define and ship products with AI agents, and who help make AURA the platform the whole company runs on.

Your **Digital Product Builder** role and your **CS-into-Aura business case** sit exactly at the join of the last two arrows. That's not a coincidence — it's why the role exists.

## 5. What This Means For You (interview leverage)

1. **Show you understand the pivot.** "You've said you're shifting from office-only to a global hydration ecosystem — BOLD, the US debut, AURA as the connective layer. That's the context I'd want my work to serve." Instant credibility.
2. **Tie your role to the efficiency goal.** The reason they want builders-with-AI is the <100-people ambition. Position yourself as someone who *multiplies* output — "I ship products with AI agents, which is exactly the leverage a lean team needs."
3. **Your CS business case = their platform goal.** Getting CS working in Aura is a small instance of their big goal (run the company on our own platform, powered by data). Frame the case that way and it stops being a random exercise — it's a scale-model of their strategy.
4. **Sustainability-as-data aligns.** Their North Star counts bottles saved; your MPG Master counted certified environmental impact. Same "impact you can measure" DNA.
5. **Honest gaps to ask about (specialist/founder round):** the exact 2026 revenue target and the US go-to-market plan aren't public. Good questions: "What does winning in 2026 look like in numbers for you?" and "Is the US a 2026 push or a 2027 one — and does that change what the digital team prioritizes?"

# AQUABLU — Company Research Brief
### Prepared for Bezawit Zerayacob Bekele · Cultural Interview with Camille (Talent Acquisition Partner) · July 2026

---

## 1. The One-Paragraph Version (memorize this)

Aquablu is an Amsterdam-based cleantech scale-up (founded 2018 by childhood friends **Marnix Stokvis, CEO** and **Marc van Zuylen, CCO**) building smart, connected water dispensers that replace single-use plastic bottles with purified, vitamin- and mineral-infused flavored water. Their flagship is the **REFILL+ Series 2**, powered by **AURA**, their IoT operating system and data platform connecting hundreds of deployed systems across **16 European countries** with roughly **150,000 daily users**. Clients include **Heineken, Adyen, Spotify, Marriott, CMS, and Compass Group**. In 2025 they were ranked **#2 fastest-growing tech company in the Netherlands (Deloitte Fast50)** and **#30 fastest-growing in Europe (Sifted 250)**, hit **profitability for the first time (April 2025)** after 300%+ YoY growth for three consecutive years, and closed a **€7 million seed round led by Frans van Houten, former CEO of Philips**. Their stated ambition: **€100M revenue with fewer than 100 employees by 2028**, and the mission slogan: *hydrate 1 billion people and save 1 billion single-use plastic bottles by 2030.*

---

## 2. The Founding Story (great cultural-interview material)

- **The surf trip.** Marnix Stokvis was on a surf trip in Cape Town, South Africa, when the tide shifted and he found himself floating in a stream of plastic bottles. His question: "We can put people on the moon — why are beverages still stuck in plastic and aluminium?"
- Back in the Netherlands he teamed up with his **childhood friend Marc van Zuylen**. Both come from **business/finance backgrounds** (Marc: MSc Strategic Management, RSM; Marnix: MSc Financial Economics, Erasmus) — non-technical founders who deliberately hired engineers and designers early and treat the business/tech split as a competitive advantage.
- **The canal stunt (2020).** They famously purified Amsterdam canal water live from a boat — pumping, filtering, mineralizing and bottling it on the spot — to prove their filtration tech. It went viral; people actually wanted to buy the water, and profits went to anti-plastic charities.
- Early roots trace to Voorhout (~2016) with water purification systems (Aquablu One / Pro, including yacht and off-grid applications) before pivoting fully to the workplace hydration platform.

**Why this matters for you:** the founders' story is about seeing a systemic problem and building a system to fix it. That maps directly to your own narrative — from food-waste thesis to configurators to MPG compliance tooling, you solve at the system level, not the surface level.

## 3. Products & Technology (know these by name)

| Product | What it is |
|---|---|
| **REFILL+ Series 2** (launched Oct 2025) | Flagship all-in-one smart dispenser for offices/commercial spaces (25+ employees). Hot (75°C/90°C), chilled, ambient, sparkling; 60+ drink combinations with zero-sugar, vitamin-infused flavors. Nano-silver filtration removes 99.9% of bacteria, contaminants, microplastics. Built-in cellular connectivity — every dispense streams to AURA in real time. Rated 4.9/5 on Trustpilot. |
| **AURA** | The operating system / intelligence platform connecting all Aquablu products and data. Gives facility managers real-time monitoring, usage insights, refill predictions, sustainability reporting. Powers flavor recommendations based on real hydration habits per location. **This is the digital heart of the company — and the surface the Digital Product Builder role touches.** |
| **Aquablu BOLD** (debuted at CES 2026, Las Vegas — their US debut) | Next-gen stand-alone connected dispenser: 5 water types, 6 flavors at varying intensities, functional boosts (electrolytes, protein, caffeine, collagen soon). Positioned as "hydration as enterprise infrastructure" for offices, gyms, hotels, high-traffic spaces. |

**Business model:** hydration-as-a-service, not equipment sales. They sell predictability — "outsourcing a daily necessity to a system that simply works." Everything designed in-house (mechanical, industrial design, electronics, embedded software, IoT), manufactured via contract manufacturers, serviced locally.

**Positioning:** They frame themselves against a **$1.5 trillion beverage industry controlled by Coca-Cola and Pepsi** — "companies selling sugar water in plastic." Marnix's line: *"What Nespresso did for coffee, we're doing for water — but sustainably and exclusively for businesses."*

- Environment described in the job ad as **"informal, direct, and collaborative"** — classic Dutch scale-up: flat, fast, blunt-but-warm.
- Perks tell you what they value: Urban Sports Club membership + sponsored gym (health/energy), healthy discounted lunches, **equity for everyone ("Aquablu for All")** — shared ownership mentality, Zwitserleven pension 50/50.
- Mission language: **"Hydrate a billion people to thrive every day and save 1 billion single-use plastic bottles before 2030."** Purpose + performance, not purpose instead of performance.

**Read between the lines:** scale-up pace ("fresh challenges every quarter," "no two days are the same"), small team with huge ambition, extreme ownership expected, low tolerance for people who wait for direction. The Digital Product Builder ad says it explicitly: "You don't hand a spec to a team and wait."

## 7. The Role in Context (why they're hiring this now)

Aquablu's digital portfolio spans three layers: **AURA in users' hands, the dispenser's digital surfaces, and the platform connecting hundreds of deployed systems** — plus internal tooling. They just launched Series 2 + AURA (Oct 2025) and BOLD (Jan 2026), are expanding into the US, and are simultaneously hiring an Android Engineer, a Senior AI Platform Engineer, an Embedded Software Engineer, and a Product Manager. Translation: the digital platform is scaling faster than the team, and they want people who can **own a product surface end-to-end and ship it themselves with AI agents** rather than adding coordination overhead. The €63–85k salary band (incl. 8% holiday allowance) confirms they're pricing this as a serious medior/senior product role.

## 8. Talking Points That Connect YOU to THEM

1. **Physical-digital products are literally your career.** Aquablu = hardware (dispensers) + IoT + cloud platform + client-facing digital surfaces. Packhunt = physical manufacturing (rails, partition walls) + parametric digital twins + cloud configurators. You've spent 5 years exactly at this intersection — it's their "nice to have" #1 (connected hardware/IoT/physical-digital) and #2 (B2B/partner-facing products).
2. **AI-native building.** Their loudest current identity marker. NoShowShield — where AI agents were your primary collaborator across specs, code, and testing, and you owned framing, validation, and explainability — is precisely the proof they ask for ("We'll ask for the demo, and how your setup works").
3. **Sustainability with teeth.** MPG Master isn't feel-good sustainability — it's legally mandated environmental compliance, built with the official Dutch body (MRPI), in production, 30–50 reports/month. Aquablu's sustainability is also measurable (bottles saved, tracked in AURA). You both do impact-as-data, not impact-as-marketing.
4. **Data-driven product decisions.** Their AURA pitch is "we see what people drink, when, and how much." Your Walraven story ("usage data showed 2 of 7 tools were used → interviews → killed 5") is the same muscle. The job ad even says: "Kill ideas that don't earn their place."
5. **Scale-up temperament.** They want people who plan, prioritise and execute without direction. You created your own PO role by pitching the CEO. That story is almost custom-made for this company.
6. **Dutch context.** You've done inburgering, you know Dutch professional directness, you've worked with Dutch manufacturers (Walraven, Systemflex) — you already operate in their world.
